## Brand & Style

The design system projects mission-critical precision, orbital velocity, and deep-space telemetry intelligence. Built for flight operations commanders, quantum avionics engineers, and orbital tracking operators, the aesthetic synthesizes high-density data visualization with cutting-edge aerospace instrumentation. 

The visual personality combines:
- **Technical Rigor:** Strict, calibrated alignments reminiscent of modern spacecraft heads-up displays (HUD) and flight management terminals.
- **Luminous Atmospheric Depth:** Deep cosmic midnight backgrounds contrasted against hyper-luminescent signal channels that command focal priority without causing optic fatigue.
- **Glassmorphic Spatial Stratification:** Multi-layered translucent panels that mirror cockpit multi-function displays (MFDs), utilizing dynamic light refraction to establish functional grouping.

This style fuses **Futuristic High-Density Data Avionics** with **Quantum Glassmorphism**. The visual output delivers effortless situational awareness under high-velocity cognitive loads, elevating complex telemetry from raw metrics into instinctive visual cognition.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly for dark-field operation to optimize legibility during critical low-light control room monitoring.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#00F0FF` - Electric Cyan):** Active vectors, targeted flight paths, laser telemetry readouts, primary action controls, and high-frequency communication pulses.
- **Secondary (`#FF9F1C` - Hyper-Luminescent Neon Amber):** Real-time warnings, engine thrust vector thresholds, trajectory variance, and caution-state indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#7928CA` - Quantum Indigo / Violet):** Quantum computational routing, orbital harmonics, sub-atomic sensor calibration, and auxiliary network states.
- **Neutral (`#0B132B` - Obsidian Deep Space):** Structural background layers, panel backdrops, radar sweep grids, and telemetry matrices.

### Functional Roles
- **Base Canvas:** Deepest obsidian void (`#060B18`) providing near-infinite contrast ratios.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Container Panel):** Translucent deep navy (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.72)`) backed with optical blur.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Elevated Telemetry Block):** Layered cosmic slate (`rgba(16, 28, 64, 0.85)`).
- **Signal Accents & State Alerts:**
  - **Nominal/Online:** Bright Quantum Emerald (`#05FFA1`) for uncompromised orbital alignment.
  - **Critical Alert:** Ion Crimson (`#FF0055`) for hardware fault and trajectory deviation.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - High Emphasis: Polar White (`#F0F6FC`) at 100% opacity.
  - Medium/Data Readouts: Aero Steel (`#8B9BB4`) at 85% opacity.
  - Micro Annotations/Guides: Sub-Orbital Muted (`#415173`) at 60% opacity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces immediate differentiation between structural narratives and real-time numeric precision.

- **Headlines & Structural Titles (`Space Grotesk`):** Engineered geometric curves that deliver a technical, aerospace-grade authority.
- **Body & Operational Content (`Inter`):** Selected for rapid ocular scanning across sustained tracking sessions, eliminating visual distraction.
- **Telemetry Readouts, Coordinates & Metrics (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-width characters ensure tabular figures never jitter during high-frequency live stream renders (e.g., dynamic Mach counts, GPS coords, and quantum state updates).

All uppercase labels (`label-caps` and `label-mono`) use deliberate tracking (letter-spacing) to guarantee legibility when rendered against low-contrast radar layers or within micro-sized status pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a disciplined **12-column modular fluid avionics grid** designed to accommodate dense telemetry widgets alongside responsive 3D tracking projections.

### Grid Configuration
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) exterior safety margins. Max container constraint is uncapped for multi-display flight deck setups, expanding cleanly up to 4K displays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) outer margins. Side panels collapse into dockable HUD drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) outer margins. Telemetry parameters transition into vertically stacked, swipe-navigable cards.

### Spacing Principles
- Density is prioritized: telemetry components utilize compact 4px (`space-xs`) and 8px (`space-sm`) baselines to package high-frequency indicators into operational viewport sectors.
- Spatial grouping operates on strict nesting: items within a module use `space-xs` to `space-sm`, modules within a panel use `space-md`, and independent telemetry panels separate by `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated not through traditional drop shadows, but through **quantum luminescent stratification and frosted optical glassmorphism**.

### Layer Strata
1. **Base Layer (Orbital Canvas):** Pitch-black cosmic ground (`#060B18`) intersected with vector coordinate axes and faint celestial radar radial rings rendered in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.04)`.
2. **Level 1 Panels (MFD Flight Displays):** 
   - Fill: `rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.65)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px) saturate(160%)`
   - Border: 1px continuous outline in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
3. **Level 2 Modals & Critical Overlays:**
   - Fill: `rgba(16, 28, 64, 0.85)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(32px)`
   - Border: 1px continuous outline in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`
   - Ambient Aura: Multi-layered photonic glow: `0 0 24px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15), 0 0 64px rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.1)`.

### Optical Border Accents
Panels utilize micro-corner tick marks and localized gradient highlights. When a flight vector enters an active or critical state, the bounding 1px border dynamically pulses via linear-gradient sweeps driven by the active signal color (Cyan, Amber, or Violet).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **sharp, military-grade aerodynamic silhouette** with subtle corner dampening (`roundedness: 1`). 

- **Containers & Glass Panels:** 0.25rem (4px) to 0.5rem (8px) edge fillets to evoke CNC-milled instrument bezels.
- **Flight Badges & Telemetry Pills:** Full encapsulation (`rounded-full`) exclusively used for real-time status chips and dynamic vector states to simulate illuminated pilot annunciator lights.
- **Cut Corners & Chamfers:** Precision telemetry callouts and HUD reticle overlays feature 45-degree chamfered edges (implemented via clip-path masks) to underscore high-tech avionics heritage.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Flight Vector Button:** High-visibility Cyan core (`#00F0FF`) with dark obsidian text (`#060B18`). Interactive hover triggers an outer cyan photon bloom (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), electric cyan hover transition.
- **Tertiary Quantum Command:** Translucent purple wash (`rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.2)`), bordered in vivid violet (`#7928CA`), reserved for computational route optimization.

### 2. Telemetry Status Chips & Flight Pills
- Rounded pill containers housing an animated breathing status dot (2Hz pulsation).
- States:
  - `NOMINAL`: Emerald aura with dark glass fill.
  - `QUANTUM LOCK`: Violet wash with active orbital trace.
  - `THRUST CAUTION`: Neon Amber fill (`#FF9F1C`) with flashing glyph.

### 3. Flight Data Cards & MFD Panels
- Built with standard Level 1 glassmorphism.
- Features high-contrast header sections with micro monospace coordinate tags (`LAT/LONG`, `ALT`, `MACH`).
- Bottom edge features a micro-gradient track displaying data bus load.

### 4. Checkboxes & Avionics Toggles
- Square, razor-sharp check indicators featuring glowing cyan tick vectors.
- Binary Flight Switches: Skeuomorphic-derived slide toggles with neon illumination channels showing active circuit continuity.

### 5. Input Fields & Waypoint Coordinates
- Background: `rgba(6, 11, 24, 0.6)`.
- Border: 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, transitioning to 1px `#00F0FF` with a subtle inner glow upon keyboard capture.
- Text: Always monospace (`JetBrains Mono`) for coordinate, altitude, and azimuth inputs.

### 6. Specialized Telemetry Components
- **Radar Reticle Overlay:** Concentric vector circles with rotating gradient sweep lines (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Quantum Routing Bar:** Segmented progress matrix tracking quantum node calculations, color-shifting from deep indigo through radiant electric cyan.
